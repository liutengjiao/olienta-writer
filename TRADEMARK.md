# Olienta Trademark And Brand Policy

This policy explains how the Olienta name, logo, domain, and official distribution identity may be used.

## Protected Brand Assets

The following brand assets are not licensed under the project's open source software license:

- The `Olienta` name.
- The `Olienta Writer` product name.
- The Olienta logo, icon, wordmark, and visual identity.
- The official domain: `https://olienta.vip`.
- Official store listings, official installer signatures, official screenshots, and official support materials.

The project source code is licensed separately under the license in `LICENSE`. That license does not grant permission to impersonate the official Olienta project or to use the Olienta brand in a confusing way.

## Allowed Uses

You may use the Olienta name to:

- Refer truthfully to the original project.
- State that your work is based on, compatible with, or derived from Olienta Writer.
- Link to the official project website or repository.
- Discuss, review, teach, or document the software.

Examples:

- "A fork of Olienta Writer"
- "Compatible with Olienta Writer project files"
- "Based on Olienta Writer, with local modifications"

## Uses That Require Permission

You need written permission before you:

- Publish an app, installer, website, service, or store listing using the name `Olienta` or `Olienta Writer` as if it were official.
- Use the Olienta logo, icon, or official screenshots to market a third-party build.
- Sell a third-party build in a way that suggests it is produced, endorsed, signed, or maintained by the official Olienta project.
- Register domains, social accounts, app store names, package names, or trademarks that are confusingly similar to Olienta.
- Remove or replace official support links with misleading payment, support, or update channels while keeping the official brand presentation.

## Forks And Third-Party Builds

Forks and third-party builds are welcome when they follow the open source license and are clearly identified.

A third-party build should use a distinct name and should clearly state:

- It is not the official Olienta Writer release.
- Who maintains it.
- Where its source code is available.
- What changes were made compared with the official version.

Do not call a fork "official Olienta", "Olienta Pro", "Olienta Writer Official", or any similar name without permission.
